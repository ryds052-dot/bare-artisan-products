Apothecary Standards & Rituals

What makes a body butter different from a lotion? +

Body butters are typically richer and thicker than lotions. Bare Artisan's body butter blends shea butter with coconut oil for a more substantial texture. Check each product's ingredient list and patch test new products if you have sensitive skin.

How do I qualify for Complimentary Shipping? +

All orders of $30.00 USD or more automatically receive complimentary shipping. A single jar of Bare Dream Cream or Bare Rosemary Body Butter ($30.00 USD) unlocks free delivery immediately.
