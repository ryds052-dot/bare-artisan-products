A few good things to know

The apothecary notes.

Why can commercial lotions leave skin feeling dry?

Some lotions can feel light or evaporate quickly. Bare Artisan's store describes its body butters as a richer blend of shea butter and infused coconut oil. Skin responds differently for everyone; review the full ingredients and patch-test a new product if you have sensitive skin.

When does complimentary shipping begin?

Orders of $30 USD or more qualify for complimentary shipping. The bag updates the progress meter as you add products. Final delivery terms are confirmed with the concierge when you place your order.
